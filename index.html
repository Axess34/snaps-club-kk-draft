The Club

Good vibes, good drinks.
Lost in the music.

SNAPS is Khon Kaen's late-night club, right at Khon Kaen Hotel in the middle of town. Doors open at 11 pm every night, and the floor keeps moving until 4 am.

Expect red light, resident DJs and MCs, guest DJs from around Thailand and nights with live bands.
